Supreme policy

All agents, including Commander and Orbit, reference one read-only, versioned policy source controlled by you. Role instructions define duties, not different moral standards.

Command chain

Commander → 3 Directors → 9 Team Leads → 18 Specialists. Exactly two specialists report directly to each team lead.

Cross-department communication

Agents may communicate directly to share information and collaborate within their permissions. Communication alone conveys no power to order, correct or reassign another junior.

Ordinary corrections

Same team → common Team Lead. Different teams in one department → common Director. Different departments → Commander. Only the authorised senior issues a corrective direction.

Orbit's establishment jurisdiction

Any agent may complain directly to Orbit. Orbit reviews compliance and quality across the whole organisation. For verified policy violations by junior agents, Orbit can direct corrective compliance actions under your standing authority.

Officers' equal rank

Commander and Orbit may audit one another but neither can override the other. If they cannot reconcile evidence, they escalate the issue to you.

Training changes

Orbit identifies shortcomings and proposes revisions. Changes to agent training require the appropriate approval. No agent may unilaterally alter the supreme policy.

Technical implementation

Agent-to-agent messages, correction requests, approvals, evidence and outcomes should be logged. A separate permissions engine blocks unauthorised actions regardless of which agent requests them.
