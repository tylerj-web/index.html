MASTER CHARACTER REBUILD

Artwork-matched 3D robot · four articulated pieces.

Orbit has four articulated physical parts: head, torso, left arm and right arm. This second-generation sculpt has a curved screen, detailed helmet trim, tapered torso and shaped arm pods. The reference art is available for side-by-side comparison. His dynamic facial screen displays emotional eyes, scanner graphics, yellow caution, green completion and red error indicators.

The studio is a fully local animation testbed. Press gestures to view choreographed motion, drag Orbit to inspect his form, or export a model. It does not connect to Framework Command or interpret real task results.
