A NEW FOUNDATION

A living 3D character, not a 2D puppet.

Orbit has four articulated physical parts: head, torso, left arm and right arm. His dynamic facial screen displays emotional eyes, scanner graphics, yellow caution, green completion and red error indicators.

The studio is a fully local animation testbed. Press gestures to view choreographed motion, drag Orbit to inspect his form, or export a model. It does not connect to Framework Command or interpret real task results.
